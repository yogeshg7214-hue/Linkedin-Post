Trip planning takes six hours. It should take six minutes.

Last trip I planned for 6 hours. Tabs open: flights, hotels, things to do, food, weather, map. Copy paste into doc. Missed details.

Next trip I did in 6 minutes with one AI flow. Same research, 10 times faster.

4 steps that actually work for personal trip planning👇

1. Gather, not 20 tabs:

> Before: Open 20 tabs for flights, hotels, things to do, food. Copy paste manually into doc.
> After: Perplexity prompt - Plan 3 day trip to Jaipur in August. Give me flights from Delhi, 3 hotel options under 5000 per night with links, 5 things to do, 3 food places, weather. Add source links.
> Time: 2 minutes vs 2 hours. Sources included.

2. Compare, not just list:

> Before: List of hotels with price, no comparison
> After: Claude prompt - Take these 3 hotel options. Compare by price, location, reviews, distance to city center. Give table with winner for location, winner for price, winner for reviews.
> Time: 1 minute. Decision done.

3. Itinerary that fits real life, not generic:

> Before: Generic itinerary - Day 1 city tour, Day 2 museum
> After: ChatGPT prompt - Here is my style - I like food, not museums, wake up late, want one chill cafe per day. Create itinerary using hotel and things to do list above. Short lines.
> Result: Itinerary that matches how you actually travel.

4. Log and share, not copy paste again:

> Before: Copy itinerary into doc, share with family on WhatsApp, they ask same questions again
> After: Make takes final itinerary and creates shareable Google Doc plus checks weather daily and sends WhatsApp reminder with what to pack.
> Time: 1 minute. No rework.

Stack: Perplexity, Claude, ChatGPT, Make, Google Docs. All free or cheap. No code.

I planned last Jaipur trip in 6 minutes. Old way 6 hours. Same trip, less stress.

Trip planning is not about more tabs. It is about one prompt that gives you sources, comparison, and itinerary that fits you.
