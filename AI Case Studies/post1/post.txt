A support team cut response time by 70 percent.

A 12 person support team handled 400 tickets daily. Average response time 6 hours. CSAT dropping.

They did not hire more people. They rebuilt workflow with AI.

Response time went from 6 hours to 1 hour 48 minutes. 70 percent cut. Same team.

4 steps they actually used👇

1. Classify, not read every ticket manually:

> Before: Agent opens every ticket, decides priority, tags, routes. 45 seconds per ticket.
> After: Claude classifies - billing, technical, refund, urgent. Auto tags and routes to right person.
> Time saved: 30 seconds per ticket, 3 hours daily for team.

2. Draft reply in brand voice, not from scratch:

> Before: Agent writes same reply 20 times a day, different wording, inconsistent tone
> After: ChatGPT drafts using 10 best past replies as style. Short lines. Empathy plus solution plus next step.
> Time saved: 2 minutes per ticket.

3. Knowledge base that answers before ticket:

> Before: Same 20 questions asked daily, answered manually each time
> After: Perplexity style search over help docs embedded in widget. Customer gets answer instantly without ticket.
> Result: 22 percent fewer tickets created.

4. Follow up that does not get missed:

> Before: Agent forgets to follow up after 24 hours. Ticket reopens angry.
> After: Make checks if no reply in 24 hours. Auto creates follow up task and Slack reminder. CSAT up.

Stack: Zendesk, Claude, ChatGPT, Make, Airtable. Built in 2 days. No extra hire.

Result: Same 12 people, 70 percent faster response, CSAT up 18 percent.

What changed was not more people. It was removing steps agents should not do manually.
