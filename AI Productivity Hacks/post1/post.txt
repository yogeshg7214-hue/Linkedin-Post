4 hacks that actually save 10 hours weekly👇

1. Research with sources, not 20 tabs:

> Before: Open 20 tabs, copy paste, verify manually. 3 hours gone.
> After: Perplexity gives answers with real links. Save thread by topic. Reuse.
> Time saved: 3 hours weekly on client research.

2. Long docs to bullets in 90 seconds:

> Before: 40 page PDF takes 2 hours to read
> After: Drop in Claude. Prompt: Give me key points, what is missing, and 3 angles for LinkedIn. Bullets only.
> Time saved: 2.5 hours on notes cleanup.

3. Meetings that write themselves:

> Before: Rewatch 1 hour call to find action items. 45 minutes.
> After: Tactiq live transcript plus summary plus action items auto to CRM.
> Time saved: 3 hours weekly on meeting follow ups.

4. Ideas to posts while you sleep:

> Before: Idea in head, blank doc, 2 hours writing
> After: Voice note to Notion, Whisper transcribes, Claude cleans, ChatGPT formats, Make schedules.
> Time saved: 9 minutes per post vs 2 hours.
