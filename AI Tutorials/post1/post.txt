Your inbox eats two hours a day.

I tracked my inbox for 14 days. Two hours daily on sorting, labeling, drafting replies.

Same emails again and again. Invoices, meeting requests, follow ups, cold pitches.

I automated 80 percent of it with one flow in one afternoon.

4 steps that actually work for Gmail automation👇

1. Label, not read everything:

> Before: Open every email, decide, drag to folder. 45 minutes morning.
> After: Gmail filter plus AI classifies - invoice, meeting, lead, newsletter. Auto labels.
> Time saved: 30 minutes daily just on sorting.

2. Extract, not copy paste:

> Before: Copy invoice number, amount, due date into sheet manually
> After: Make watches Gmail for label invoice. Claude extracts number, amount, due date, vendor. Adds to Sheet.
> Time saved: 20 minutes per invoice. No missed fields.

3. Draft, not write from scratch:

> Before: Write same reply 5 times a day - meeting confirmation, follow up, thank you
> After: ChatGPT drafts in my voice using 3 of my best replies as style. Short lines. One question at end.
> Time saved: 40 minutes daily on replies.

4. Follow up, not forget:

> Before: Forget to follow up after 3 days. Lead goes cold.
> After: Make checks if no reply in 3 days. Auto creates task in Airtable and Slack reminder.
> Result: No lead missed. Follow up rate 100 percent.

Stack: Gmail, Make, Claude, ChatGPT, Airtable, Slack. No code needed. Cost 29 dollars for Make.

Start with one email type you hate most. Automate that one. Then add next.
