# AI Prompts

This category is dedicated to the art and science of Prompt Engineering. It's where you share the specific "magic words" that get the best results from AI.

## Where to share "Top Prompts":
I have created a specific file for this: `categories/ai-prompts/top-prompts.md`. You can list your most successful, battle-tested prompts there.

## Ideas for Prompt Posts:
- **The "Persona" Prompt**: How to make the AI act like a senior expert.
- **Chain of Thought**: Prompts that force the AI to explain its reasoning.
- **Few-Shot Prompting**: Providing examples within the prompt to improve accuracy.
- **Negative Prompts**: Telling the AI what *not* to do.
