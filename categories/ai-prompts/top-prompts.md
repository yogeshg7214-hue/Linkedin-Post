# Top Prompts Collection

Share your best-performing prompts here. These are high-value for LinkedIn posts!

### 1. The "Expert Consultant" Prompt
**Goal:** Get high-level strategic advice.
**Prompt:** "Act as a [Role, e.g., Senior Marketing Strategist] with 20 years of experience. I am going to give you a problem. First, ask me 5 clarifying questions to understand my context better. Then, provide a 3-step solution."

### 2. The "Code Reviewer" Prompt
**Goal:** Improve code quality and find bugs.
**Prompt:** "Review the following code for security vulnerabilities, performance bottlenecks, and readability. Suggest specific refactorings and explain why they are better."

### 3. The "Content Transformer" Prompt
**Goal:** Turn a long transcript or article into a LinkedIn post.
**Prompt:** "I will provide a text. Extract the 3 most controversial or surprising points and turn them into a punchy LinkedIn post starting with a hook that challenges a common industry belief."
