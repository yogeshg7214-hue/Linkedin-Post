# AI Automation (No-Code)

Building systems that work while you sleep using AI and automation tools.

## Ideas:
- **Connecting AI to Your Inbox**: Automated email sorting and drafting.
- **AI + Make.com/Zapier**: Building complex workflows without writing code.
- **Automated Content Distribution**: How to turn 1 video into 10 posts using AI.
- **Building an AI Research Assistant**: Automating data gathering from the web.
