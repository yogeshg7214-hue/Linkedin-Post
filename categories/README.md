# LinkedIn Post Categories for AI

This folder contains different categories for LinkedIn posts focused on Artificial Intelligence.

## Categories

1. **AI Cheatsheets**: High-value guides (e.g., Claude prompt engineering, ChatGPT master cheatsheet).
2. **AI Guides**: Comprehensive deep-dives into specific AI topics (e.g., "The Ultimate Guide to RAG").
3. **AI Tutorials**: Step-by-step "how-to" instructions (e.g., "How to build a chatbot in 10 minutes").
4. **AI Courses**: Reviews, recommendations, and learning paths.
5. **AI Workflow**: Integrating AI into professional workflows.
6. **AI Tools**: Discovering and reviewing new AI tools.
7. **AI Coding & Development**: Technical posts about building AI models and using APIs.
8. **AI Productivity Hacks**: Quick tips for better productivity.
9. **AI Case Studies**: Real-world examples of successful AI implementation.
10. **AI Resources**: Curated lists of newsletters, books, and top AI influencers to follow.
11. **AI Career Tips**: Advice on getting hired in AI, transitioning roles, and skill building.
12. **AI Industry-Specific**: How AI is transforming specific sectors like Marketing, HR, or Finance.
13. **AI Myths vs. Reality**: Debunking common misconceptions about AI capabilities.
14. **AI Agents**: Exploring the future of autonomous agents and how to use them.
15. **AI Prompts**: Specific prompt templates and engineering techniques (including "Top Prompts").
16. **AI Design & Creative**: Visual AI tools like Midjourney, DALL-E, and video generation.
17. **AI Model Comparisons**: Head-to-head battles (e.g., Claude vs. ChatGPT vs. Gemini).
18. **Open Source AI**: Focus on Llama, Mistral, and running AI locally (Ollama).
19. **AI Soft Skills & Mindset**: The human side—AI literacy, critical thinking, and adaptation.
20. **AI Automation (No-Code)**: Using Zapier/Make to build AI-powered automated systems.
21. **AI for Personal Life**: Travel, health, finance, and learning using AI.
