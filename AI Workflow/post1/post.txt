Content creators drown in a ten step process.

Idea in head. Research 2 hours. Write 2 hours. Edit 1 hour. Design 1 hour. Schedule. Repurpose. Burnout.

I mapped the process. 10 steps. 8 steps were repeat work, not creative.

I rebuilt it into 4 steps that run while I sleep.

4 steps that actually work for creators👇

1. Capture, not remember:

> Before: Ideas lost because not written down
> After: Voice note or saved link dropped into one Notion database. 10 seconds. That is only manual step.
> Tool: Notion plus Whisper for transcription.

2. Clean and extract:

> Before: Messy transcript takes 1 hour to clean
> After: Claude prompt - Take raw text. Remove filler. Extract 3 key points, one story, one lesson. Keep my words.
> Time: 90 seconds total.

3. Format to your style:

> Before: Write from scratch, guess hook strength
> After: ChatGPT prompt - Here are 3 of my best posts [paste]. Analyze sentence length, hook style. Format this idea same style. Short lines. 14 plus paragraphs.
> Time: 2 minutes. Output ready to post.

4. Schedule and repurpose:

> Before: Post manually, forget to repurpose
> After: Make pushes to Taplio. Hook scored 1 to 10. If above 7, schedules 9 am and creates carousel outline in Canva. Also logs as tweet thread and newsletter hook in Airtable.
> Time: 1 minute review.

Result: Old way 2 hours per post, 3 posts per week. New way 9 minutes per post, 32 posts scheduled in 3 hour block.

68 hours saved last month. Same creator. Different system.

Start with step 1 today. Drop one voice note into Notion.

Save this workflow for your next content batch.
