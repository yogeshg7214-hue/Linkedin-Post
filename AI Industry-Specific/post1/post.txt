Marketing teams do the same work twice.

A marketing team of 5 spent Monday doing research. Tuesday writing same research into posts. Same work twice.

They fixed it with one AI workflow that turns research into 5 formats automatically.

4 steps they use daily now👇

1. Research once, not twice:

> Before: Research for blog, then research again for LinkedIn, then again for newsletter
> After: Perplexity research with sources saved once in Notion. Single source of truth.
> Time saved: 3 hours weekly on duplicate research.

2. Extract angles, not just summary:

> Before: Summary only, then brainstorm angles manually
> After: Claude prompt - From this research, give me 3 angles for LinkedIn, 1 for blog, 1 for email. Each with hook idea.
> Time saved: 1 hour per piece.

3. Draft in brand voice, not generic:

> Before: Generic AI drafts that need full rewrite
> After: ChatGPT prompt - Here are 3 of our best posts [paste]. Rewrite this angle in same voice. Short lines. One idea per line.
> Time saved: 45 minutes per post.

4. Repurpose automatically, not manually:

> Before: Manually turn blog into tweets, LinkedIn, newsletter
> After: Make takes approved blog and creates LinkedIn post, 5 tweets, newsletter hook, and carousel outline in Canva. Auto logs in Airtable.
> Time saved: 2 hours per blog.

Result: Same team of 5, 3 times more content, 8 hours saved weekly. No extra hire.

Marketing is not about creating more. It is about reusing what you already researched better.
